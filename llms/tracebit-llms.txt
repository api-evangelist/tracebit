# Tracebit

> Tracebit is a security-canary (deception) platform backed by Accel. It deploys realistic decoy credentials, secrets, and identities — canaries — across cloud (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud), identity (Okta), Kubernetes, CI/CD, and workstations, and raises high-fidelity alerts the moment an attacker touches one. The free Community Edition exposes a public REST API and CLI.
